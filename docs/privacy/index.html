01Data We Collect

BEYSCO Technology Integrator ("BEYSCO", "we", "us") collects information you provide when you use our services, submit technical inquiries, or interact with our infrastructure platforms. This includes:

Identity data
full name, job title, company name
Contact data
work email address, phone number
Technical data
IP address, browser type, operating system, usage logs
Communication data
messages, project specifications, and correspondence

02How We Use Data

We use collected data strictly to provide and improve our services:

  • To respond to technical inquiries and route requests to the appropriate engineering division
  • To deliver contracted integration, infrastructure, and consulting services
  • To send service updates, security notices, and administrative messages
  • To comply with legal obligations and regulatory requirements
  • To analyze usage patterns and improve platform performance

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

03Data Sharing

BEYSCO may share data with trusted partners solely to deliver our services:

Service providers
cloud infrastructure vendors, analytics providers, and CRM platforms operating under strict data processing agreements
Technology partners
other integration partners, only to the extent required for the contracted service
Legal authorities
when required by applicable law, court order, or regulatory mandate

04Security

We implement enterprise-grade security measures including zero-trust architecture, end-to-end encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access controls, and continuous monitoring. All personnel handling personal data are bound by confidentiality obligations.

Despite these measures, no data transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. You assume responsibility for maintaining the security of any credentials you use to access our platforms.

05Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right of access
obtain a copy of data we hold about you
Right to rectification
correct inaccurate or incomplete data
Right to erasure
request deletion of your data
Right to restriction
limit how we process your data
Right to portability
receive your data in a structured format
Right to object
object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@beysco.com.

06Cookies

We use essential cookies required for site functionality and optional analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with our platform. You may disable non-essential cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of our services.
