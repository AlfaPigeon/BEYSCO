Work with us

Good people are welcome to write anytime — here is how it works when they do.

We're a five-person studio that ships production systems end to end. A new person changes what a team this size is — so we hire rarely, and carefully.

What we look for is simple: people who have shipped real things, explain their decisions plainly, and reach for boring tools before clever ones.

Open rolesNone right now. When that changes, they'll be listed here first — until then, the door below is open.

How we hire

  1. 01

    Write to us

    A short email: what you've shipped, what you want to build next. No cover letter theatre.

  2. 02

    One real conversation

    A call about your work — how you built it, what broke, what you'd do differently. No whiteboard puzzles.

  3. 03

    A paid trial task

    A small, real piece of work from an actual project. Paid, scoped to days, done on your schedule.

  4. 04

    An answer within a week

    Either way — with reasons, not a template.
